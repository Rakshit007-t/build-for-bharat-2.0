# Analytics Objective

## Working objective

How can job-market demand, technical skill outcomes, and workforce traits be analyzed to provide transparent and appropriately bounded talent and workforce intelligence using the four organizer-provided datasets?

## Analytical questions

1. What roles, skills, locations, experience requirements, and salary values occur in the two supplied job-posting datasets, and where do missingness or inconsistent formats limit interpretation?
2. Do the five supplied JDS skill features support out-of-fold prediction of the dataset's salary-hike target compared with simple baselines?
3. Do the five supplied SDS trait features support out-of-fold prediction of the dataset's organizational success label compared with simple baselines?
4. What cautious operational questions can stakeholders take from these results, and what additional data or validation would be needed before use?

## Evidence types

- **Descriptive analysis:** aggregate frequencies, distributions, and relationships within the provided job postings.
- **Predictive modelling:** stratified cross-validation for the supplied binary labels, reported with baseline comparisons and class balance.
- **Association:** correlations or feature importances describe observed patterns; they do not identify causal mechanisms.
- **Business implications:** proposed uses are hypotheses for review, not effects demonstrated by this observational analysis.

No conclusions or metrics are asserted in this objective document. Results are generated only after local execution on the organizer files.
