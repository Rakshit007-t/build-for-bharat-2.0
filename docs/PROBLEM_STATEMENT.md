# Problem Statement

Workforce and talent decisions are often made with incomplete views of advertised demand, skill outcomes, and organizational labels. The supplied datasets offer several lenses on these questions, but each has a limited scope and must be interpreted in context.

Ghost Skills builds a local analytics prototype that describes technical job postings, evaluates whether the JDS Skill Traits data can support a cautious classification of its supplied salary-hike label, and examines whether SDS personality traits are associated with the supplied organizational success label.

The prototype is not a resume verifier, candidate ranking system, causal study, or universal hiring model. It does not infer that a skill causes salary growth or that a personality trait causes success. Predictions are limited to the supplied labels and sample; descriptive posting patterns are not a complete labor-market census.
